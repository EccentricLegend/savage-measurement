    <p><strong>Last updated: July 2026</strong></p>

    <h2>1. Information We Do Not Collect</h2>
    <p>The "Savage Measurement" Chrome extension operates entirely on your local device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, or analytics data.</p>

    <h2>2. Local Processing</h2>
    <p>All screen measurements, calculations, and element interactions are processed locally within your browser. No data is sent to external servers.</p>
